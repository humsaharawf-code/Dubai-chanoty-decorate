About Our Business

Dubai Challenge Decor is your trusted choice for professional POP False Ceiling, Gypsum Board, and Wallpaper installation. If you are searching from Pakistan, we provide the best local POP Ceiling Design (پی او پی فالس سیلنگ), Gypsum Board Roof Work, and interior decoration services.

For international clients searching from Europe and the UK, we specialize in high-quality Plasterboard, Drywall, Drylining, Suspended Ceilings, and Dropped Ceilings [1.3.4، 1.4.1، 1.4.3]. For clients in Arab and Gulf countries, we offer premium modern Gypsum Decoration (ديكورات جبس) and false ceilings (سقف مستعار / سقف كاذب) [1.1.4، 1.4.2]. With rich experience since 1997, we deliver beautiful and durable craftsmanship worldwide.
